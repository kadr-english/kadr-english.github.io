About

About KADR

KADR is a free website for learning English. It brings together lessons by CEFR level, English for IT textbooks for Grades 9 and 10, conversation clubs, and two tests that help you see where you stand.

KADR and Al-Khwarizmi Successors

KADR is part of the "Al-Khwarizmi Successors" project. The club manuals, the English for IT textbooks and the audio materials shown here belong to that project, and more materials are shared in its Telegram channel.

Open the Telegram channel

What you will find here

  • Lessons for six CEFR levels, from A1 to C2: explanations, examples, key words, a quiz, reading, listening and speaking practice.
  • English for IT textbooks for Grades 9 and 10, shown as page images with a table of contents for each unit.
  • Clubs: Speaking & Movie Club, Movie Club, Tech Talks & Debate and the Reading Club.
  • A level test (12 questions: grammar, vocabulary, reading and listening) and speaking practice (5 questions, by voice or by typing). Both give rough estimates, not official results.

Your progress and privacy

There are no accounts. Your progress is saved only in this browser on this device, so it is not shared between devices. You can clear it at any time with "Reset my progress" at the bottom of the page. Speaking test audio is not recorded or sent anywhere from this page.

Interface language

The interface can be switched between English, Russian and Uzbek. The learning materials stay in English.

Authors and contacts

The names of the authors and contact details are not published on this site. For questions and new materials, please use the project's Telegram channel.
